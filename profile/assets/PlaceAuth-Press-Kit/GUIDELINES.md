# PlaceAuth Logo Guidelines

## The system

The layered spatial-plane emblem carries the identity. The lowercase wordmark is intentionally quiet: medium weight, close tracking, and a controlled optical gap. Do not substitute the earlier badge, pin, or contour emblem.

## Color

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#061625` | Primary wordmark and dark field |
| Deep Blue | `#0D2B42` | Emblem depth |
| Paper | `#F8FAFC` | Light field and reverse treatment |
| Teal | `#20E6C8` | Left spatial plane accent |
| Sky | `#38BDF8` | Right spatial plane accent |
| Muted Blue-Gray | `#60758A` | Foundation descriptor |

## Use the right lockup

- **Horizontal lowercase:** default for websites, documents, product surfaces, signatures, and introductions.
- **Emblem only:** use only where PlaceAuth is already established or space is genuinely constrained.
- **Stacked:** use for centered, square, social, or cover applications.
- **Foundation horizontal:** use for board, governance, institutional, and organization-level communications.

## Do

- Preserve the supplied proportions and clear space.
- Use the reverse horizontal lockup on navy or comparably dark fields.
- Keep the emblem and wordmark together; do not rebuild the lockup with a different font.
- Use the provided paper or navy field exports when predictable contrast matters, especially in email clients.

## Do not

- Redraw, rotate, crop, stretch, recolor, outline, or add effects to the emblem.
- Uppercase the primary `placeauth` wordmark.
- Use the Foundation descriptor in place of the primary logo in product contexts.
- Place the navy wordmark directly over a dark, busy, or low-contrast image.
- Add competing gradients, glows, rounded badges, or promotional-card styling.
