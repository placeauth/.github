# PlaceAuth press kit

## Source of truth

`svg/placeauth-emblem-master.svg` is the approved emblem master. Do not redraw, stretch, crop, add effects, or substitute a different emblem.

## Logo use

- Use the standard transparent emblem and horizontal lockup on white or light neutral fields.
- Use the reverse transparent assets on navy or other dark fields.
- Keep clear space equal to at least one quarter of the emblem width.
- Keep the emblem intact; its planes and governed center are one mark.

## Palette

- Navy: `#061625`
- Deep navy: `#0D2B42`
- Cyan: `#20E6C8`
- Sky: `#38BDF8`
- Paper/reverse: `#F8FAFC`

## Contents

- `svg/`: approved master plus reverse emblem asset.
- `png/transparent/`: transparent emblem and horizontal lockups for web, repositories, and presentations.
- `png/social/`: GitHub avatar, X header, and Facebook cover.

The SVG master is the only editable vector source. Horizontal lockups are supplied as deployment PNGs so they do not depend on a recipient’s locally installed font.
